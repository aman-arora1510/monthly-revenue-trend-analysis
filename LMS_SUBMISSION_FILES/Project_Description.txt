EXECUTIVE ABSTRACT & PROBLEM STATEMENT:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In IT professional services, financial velocity dictates operational stability, delivery margin health, and cash flow. Novatech Solutions previously suffered from a 14-day month-end reporting latency caused by fragmented spreadsheets, timesheets, and accounting silos. This legacy process obscured gross delivery margin overruns, delayed accounts receivable collections (DSO > 45 days), and left single-client revenue concentration (>15% revenue dependency) completely unmonitored.

To solve this, the "Monthly Revenue Trend Analysis Platform" provides an automated, sub-second financial intelligence pipeline. Backed by an embedded SQLite Kimball-style Star Schema (data/app.db) and a responsive multipage Streamlit web platform with Tailwind CSS styling, the system processes 240 clean transactional invoices spanning 24 continuous months (Jan 2024 to Dec 2025) with zero referential foreign key violations.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MATHEMATICAL FORMULATIONS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. MoM Growth % = ((Revenue_t - Revenue_{t-1}) / Revenue_{t-1}) * 100
2. Budget Variance = Actual Revenue - Target Budget
3. Target Attainment % = (Actual Revenue / Target Budget) * 100
4. Gross Margin % = ((Billed Amount - Direct Delivery Cost) / Billed Amount) * 100 (Target >= 35%)
5. Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) = (Accounts Receivable / Total Credit Sales) * 30 (Target <= 45 days)
6. Client Concentration Share % = (Client Revenue / Total Monthly Revenue) * 100 (Trigger alert if >= 15%)
7. Holt-Winters Triple Smoothing: Level, Trend, Seasonality with 95% Confidence Interval
